ERA5Viewer Connect

1. Connect to the University of Bath VPN in your usual way if off campus.
   Complete the normal university MFA step there.
2. Extract the download for your operating system. Keep all its files together.
3. Open ERA5ViewerConnect.exe (Windows), ERA5ViewerConnect.app (Mac), or
   ERA5ViewerConnect (Linux).
4. Enter your Bath username and password, then choose Connect and open viewer.
5. Keep the launcher open while using the browser. Closing it or choosing
   Disconnect closes the connection.

The launcher normally opens http://127.0.0.1:8769/ (localhost). If that port is
already occupied, it picks a free local port and opens that address instead.

Your account must have SSH access to eepc-0184.bath.ac.uk. The ERA5 reader must
already be running on that computer. Contact the maintainer if SSH login works
but the reader is unavailable. This launcher does not install or start the
reader on the remote computer.

Passwords are never saved. The VPN signs in through the university's normal
VPN/MFA interface; this app only handles the SSH connection. Additional SSH
sign-in questions, if the server requires them, appear in separate dialogs.

If Windows, macOS or a managed university device blocks this app, ask the
maintainer or Bath IT for an approved/signed build. Do not change device
security settings to work around an unexpected block.

VPN help: https://www.bath.ac.uk/guides/setting-up-vpn-on-your-device/
Source and rebuild instructions: https://github.com/tbanyard/era5viewer
